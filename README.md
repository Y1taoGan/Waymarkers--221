## Problem Statement
Tens of thousands of UBC students need somewhere to study between classes, but the usual study spaces fill up fast. During midterms and finals, library seats and lounges are often full by noon, and group study rooms are booked days ahead. Students end up wandering between buildings, studying in hallways, or giving up and going home. At the same time, hundreds of classrooms across campus sit empty for hours every day because these classrooms are only scheduled for part of the day when class is running. These classrooms are called “Ghost Rooms” that students can find and study in.

If these spaces exist, why aren’t students using them? Official booking systems cover only bookable study rooms, not general classrooms. Campus maps show where buildings are, but not when rooms are free. Wandering and checking doors wastes the time students were trying to save. The result is a campus with plenty of space that students can’t find when they need it.

## Solution Statement
Our solution aims to help students find these vacant classrooms and transform them into study spaces. We will also have a list of recommended study spaces that listens to user data. The list will contain information such as their vacancies or how to book them. Our solution will not only relieve the excessive demand on the usual study spots (e.g., the libraries and bookable study rooms) but also be a win for the students, who wouldn’t need to search everywhere to find a space to study on campus, and a win for UBC, where we are utilizing unused space and providing students with extra study spaces at no extra cost.
